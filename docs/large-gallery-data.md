# Large real gallery sources and storage

The operator's versioned VidTIMIT gallery and the engineering fixtures have separate catalogs, databases, thumbnails, network services and access paths. The fixture at port 8094 contains generated metadata and abstract tiles. Its population supports a browser or database benchmark. Accuracy estimates require independent photographs or recording sessions of real people.

| Source | Published population and independent evidence | Access and storage decision for this host |
|---|---|---|
| VidTIMIT and PaSC | VidTIMIT has 43 people across three sessions. PaSC has 293 people, 9,376 still images and 2,802 videos. | The VidTIMIT release fits the host. The 71 GB PaSC distribution requires a signed agreement, a Globus transfer and additional headroom here. [Research pilot, source terms and import](vidtimit43.md) |
| MegaFace MF2 | The University of Washington reports 672,057 identities and 4.7 million training images. Its million-photo distractor set lists 690,572 unique users under a separate protocol. | The university discontinued distribution. The old training download agreement is historical. With a hypothetical 10 KiB per image, even a compressed local copy of 4.7M images would take roughly 45 GiB before extraction or indexes. [University project status and counts](https://megaface.cs.washington.edu/) |
| Glint360K | The authors report 360,232 classes and 17,091,657 aligned face images. The release describes a training dataset. The source list supplies no independent subject-to-video/session mapping for the operator evaluation. | The authors restrict distributed training data to noncommercial research and list distribution links. Their training configuration illustrates a 140 GB RAM disk. At 8 KiB per image the payload alone would be approximately 130 GiB. The host initially had 76 GiB free and reserves 24 GiB for the pipeline. An authorized subject-disjoint subset with acquisition provenance or additional storage and a separately authorized probe set are prerequisites for a real large-gallery trial. [Publisher dataset list and terms](https://github.com/deepinsight/insightface/tree/master/recognition/_datasets_) · [Original Glint360K release](https://github.com/deepinsight/insightface/tree/master/recognition/partial_fc) · [Training configuration](https://github.com/deepinsight/insightface/blob/master/recognition/arcface_torch/configs/glint360k_r50.py) |

For a future rights-cleared, preselected import, budget the publisher's download bytes, unpacked full-resolution media, private portrait derivatives, the active gallery, the candidate gallery, SQLite index/WAL growth and the existing 24 GiB reserve separately. A 128-dimensional float32 vector uses 512 bytes. With one vector per identity the vectors alone occupy 48.8 MiB for 100,000 people and 146.5 MiB for 300,000. A planning assumption of 50 KiB per original portrait plus 8 KiB per thumbnail adds 5.5 and 16.6 GiB respectively, before the download cache, redundant versions, private video, databases or backups. Measure the candidate shard first and retain each dataset's own identity namespace.
